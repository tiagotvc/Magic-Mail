Use case: stylized-concept. NEW design concept sheet for a cartoon POSTAL INKWELL obstacle in a casual match3 game. Previous attempts looked like FLOWERPOTS and are rejected. PRIMARY REQUIREMENT: unmistakable classic desk INK BOTTLE silhouette: very squat broad rounded-square bottle body, flat stable bottom, distinct broad almost-horizontal shoulders tapering into a VERY SHORT NARROW cylindrical neck with a tiny circular pen-dipping opening. Body width is about THREE TIMES neck width. Opening diameter only about ONE QUARTER of body width. Neck is NOT flared, NO thick rolled mouth rim. NO flowerpot shape, NO vase, NO urn, NO large open bowl mouth, NO trumpet opening. Think classic antique desktop ink bottle simplified into a chunky cute Royal Match style puzzle obstacle. Purple opaque ceramic bottle, one simple warm yellow gold narrow neck collar, small gold envelope emblem on front; no large golden pedestal or bottom ring. Friendly rounded corners, simple saturated color blocks, soft cartoon highlights, restrained 2.5D toy rendering, not realistic glass/metal, no detailed textures. No quill inserted, no props. 
Exactly SIX views of SAME bottle in spacious 3-column 2-row grid, same fixed scale and viewpoint (front slightly above), reading left to right: pristine; after hit1 one crack; after hit2 branching cracks; after hit3 a small shoulder chip missing with more cracks; after hit4 a bigger side chunk missing but bottle still standing on its original base; after hit5 a low small pile of dry rounded purple ceramic fragments plus broken gold neck collar, no intact bottle. Distinct increasing damage without changing the original body proportions. ALL SIX SPRITES DRY: NO liquid, NO ink drips, NO leaks, NO puddles, NO splashes, NO droplets, NO glowing trails. Ink effects will be animated separately. No text/numbers, no board, no frame, no crown, no floor or shadows outside objects, no gradient backdrop. Actual transparent background. Concept sheet only. Prioritize the very narrow tiny bottle opening over all decorative concerns.
