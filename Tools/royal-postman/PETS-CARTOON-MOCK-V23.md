Construction like a premium Royal Match play button:
Each colored button has a SINGLE CLEARLY RAISED CENTRAL FRONT FACE, a broad smooth domed rectangular plateau with very round corners. A WIDE SLOPING COLORED BEVEL connects that raised central face to the larger colored footprint, creating obvious 3D thickness. Its upper/left shoulder is bright vivid turquoise or violet, and lower/right shoulder is DEEP SATURATED TEAL OR PURPLE. Quality: pristine raster cartoon UI painting, crisp smooth curves, confident wide colored bevels, sharp lettering, vivid readable volume at phone scale.
