# Royal Postman — quality requirements and mascot proposal

Superseded proposal notice: the current user requirements are consolidated in `ACCEPTANCE.md`. The selected discussion direction is Tamagotchi-style care with battle assistance and a dedicated pet button. The narrative delivery/griffin proposal below was not selected; its exclusion of combat is no longer applicable. No pet species or battle mechanics have been approved for implementation.

19 October 2026. The visual mockup reference is `previews/home-hud-v4-letter-coin-corrected-arms.png`. The user has not finalized production integration. The main illustration is provisional and may be replaced by a mascot system.

## User requirements

- Clean, smooth cartoon painting: no stains, unintended blotches, smears, compression marks, halos, jagged edges, malformed anatomy or generation artifacts. Deliberate broad highlights and colored shadows remain part of the approved painting; do not confuse them with noise.
- Typography must match the approved mockup: letterforms, weight, proportions, cream/plum colors, outline, short shadow, spacing and alignment. A merely similar font is not sufficient. Treat this as a fidelity requirement to verify against the reference, not as a solved font match. Text must remain an independent UI layer; generated lettering in the concept is not production-ready dynamic text.
- Preserve the approved palette, clean sunny cartoon gold, mail-envelope coin emblem, continuous red navigation and compact taller rounded-rectangle teal level button. Frame, moving center, icons and labels remain separate assets/components.
- No construction system currently desired. Do not assume the central Courier/owl image is final or build the new home around permanent character coordinates.
- Show concepts before production integration. The user is exploring a mascot mechanic connecting magic, postal delivery and a kingdom.

## Proposed direction — not yet approved

A personal magical postal companion, provisionally a tiny griffin, helps restore communication across the kingdom. A letter addressed to the player introduces the companion. It senses the intent of undelivered messages and finds recipients through magic.

Core loop: complete match-3 levels, earn progress toward a special delivery, choose between incoming letters, hand a letter to the companion, see a short delivery/reply scene, receive a keepsake and visible companion progression. Numbers and pacing are prototype assumptions to test, not final economy tuning.

The kingdom progresses through correspondents, replies, relationships and postal honors, rather than upgraded buildings. The companion remembers small player choices through expressions, tricks, keepsakes and journal entries. Cosmetic choices and habits are revisable; players can eventually see all story branches. Personality choices should not create hidden competitive advantages.

Home: companion occupies the replaceable center scene; one incoming envelope provides the next light interaction; Play remains the primary CTA. Most taps and progression keep returning to the match-3 core. Start with one memorable companion rather than a roster.

Prototype scope: one companion, three short letter chains, a few readable expression/interaction states, two learned tricks and one visible milestone. Test affection for the creature, anticipation of replies and motivation to play another level. Hunger decay, mandatory maintenance, breeding, asynchronous expeditions, social networking, combat and board-power balancing are outside the initial prototype.
